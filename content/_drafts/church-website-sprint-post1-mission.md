# Go Find a Church

[GAP: Opening conflict — what does it cost a church to have a broken, outdated, or missing website? Missed first-time visitors? Confusion about where they meet? Name the specific moment a real person hits a church site and bounces. This is your hook.]

There is no church that should be without a simple website.

Churches face a hard reality: their mission is everything that is *not* managing websites, writing copy, setting up hosting accounts, or figuring out what a domain registrar is. They were trained in seminary to steward their congregation and community. That is what they are good at. That is who they serve well.

Everything else is overhead.

Attracting the right people matters. Someone looking for a church like theirs should be able to find them. But building and maintaining a website is not in the skill set of most church staff — and it should not have to be.

That is where you come in.

[GAP: Why YOU specifically — the tech person reading this — are positioned to help. What makes a software engineer or developer the right person for this? Is it the 2-day investment? The skills that feel "basic" to you but are out of reach for a church admin?]

There is a version of this that does not require a retainer, a monthly fee, or a long-term commitment. A simple site. Done in a couple of days. Handed off completely. With documentation so anyone can pick it up after you leave.

The goal is not to build a website. The goal is to give a church a website SOP — a starting point for everything that comes after.
