What visibility do we have available to us to meet our expectations so we don't worry, wait, or wonder?

Michael Janda introduced me to the concept of avoiding the [3Ws](/glossary/#3ws) (worry, wait, wonder) in his book Burn Your Portfolio.

I call this visibility the [fishbowl experience](/glossary/#fishbowl-experience). This visibility of being able to see inside the fishbowl tells us so much about what needs to happen next. This transparency prevents the 3Ws. 

The fish may need water or food or a cleaning or we can't find the fish. The visibility is everything to help the owner take care of the fish.

So, what are some other examples where we have this fishbowl experience? 

Here's what I've come up with so far...

1. USPS/UPS package tracking
2. stock ticker
3. Domino's Pizza Tracker
4. Find My Friends (iOS app)
5. shared calendars
6. shared todo lists (Asana, Todoist, Jira, Monday.com)
7. SMS/email read status or the 💬 message bubble while someone is typing

I'm sure more are going to come to mind, but all of these are optional to subscribe to and give us enough information to make a decision. None of these are perfect either, but they help us reduce the 3Ws.

## The point

How can we provide any visibility for those that expect anything of us so that they don't have to worry, wait, or wonder?

Find a way to make the work more visible and I promise that those that work with you and depend on you will appreciate the updates you provide.
