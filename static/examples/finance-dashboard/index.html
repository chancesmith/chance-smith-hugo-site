Pay off the car loan from cash now, or keep the cushion?

Do first, sets up #2 and #3 Unblocks:Ramsey 2FOO 4Flowchart 1
  • Pay it off now: cash drops to about $1,650. Still above Ramsey's $1,000 starter fund, and the $310/mo payment frees up today.
  • Keep paying monthly: cash stays at $7,200 and reaches a 3-month fund ($8,700 at ~$2,900/mo) in about 10 months. The loan finishes on schedule.
Ramsey says pay it now. FOO and the flowchart would rather see 3 months of cash first, since 6.9% is moderate, not high, interest.
Why it matters now: every month the loan runs costs about $32 in interest. Every month without a cushion means one surprise bill lands on a credit card.
